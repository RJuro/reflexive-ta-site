How the system knows things

A working map of the instrument: how evidence becomes claims, how a researcher governs it, and where each mechanism lives in the code. Written to argue with.

01 · The system model

Two currents, four layers

Everything else in this document is downstream of one idea: evidence ascends, direction descends, and every crossing is checked. The layers are not a file hierarchy — they are altitudes of claim, and each one exists only because the layer below vouches for it.

Direction descends
L3

Focus & story

The research question and the account so far. Both versioned; the question is emergent unless pinned, and the machine may propose a revision when the material keeps pointing elsewhere.

crossing · a story paragraph without resolving anchors is dropped
L2

Findings

Cross-case claims. Standing is computed from the support beneath — never asked of the model, never set by hand. Verdicts wait for a review sitting.

crossing · a finding must name real supporting codes; invented ids are dropped
L1

Codes

Grounded observations, capped at 25 per document. Every one cites sentence ids; reuse of an existing code is preferred over minting a new one.

crossing · the grounding gate — a code citing text that isn't there is dropped
L0

The material

Transcripts with stable sentence ids and character offsets. Immutable after ingest, because everything above hangs off those offsets.

Evidence rises

The upward current is the only way anything comes to exist. Nothing at a layer may exist without anchors in the layer below, which is why any claim can be walked down to a participant's actual words — and why quote fabrication is structurally impossible rather than merely checked for: the model cites ids, and quote text is resolved from offsets at render time.

The downward current is how the researcher governs — by direction and exception, at whatever altitude they happen to be working. Direction never edits evidence. It changes how the next ascent reads.

A steer the material cannot carry comes back flagged, never silently absorbed. Agreement the system cannot ground is worthless to a researcher.
02 · What happens to one interview

From a voice recording to a claim

Two model calls per document do the analytic work. Everything else is Python, and that split is deliberate: the model judges, the code disposes.

Why so few calls: with a thinking model, reasoning is billed per call. The earlier pipeline paid that toll nineteen to twenty-five times per document by coding section-by-section. One large reasoning budget over a whole transcript is both cheaper and better-attended — with the coverage gate as the safeguard against the one real risk of reading long.

03 · The loop mechanisms

Where the two currents actually meet

Four mechanisms turn the model from a diagram into machinery. Each one is a place where direction gets tested against evidence rather than obeyed.

Check-back

Trigger Any standing researcher steer — a reframed finding, a challenge, an edited claim.

System Re-tests it against the corpus on the next synthesis and answers in three parts: what supports it, what strains it, what it could not find. A check-back where all three are empty is rejected by the validator — flattery is a bug, not a tone.

Researcher Adopts the proposed revision, or keeps their framing.

Residue & abduction

Trigger Passages no code catches; codes no finding wants; findings that strain the story.

System Surfaces them rather than smoothing them, and may propose a reframe that would accommodate them. Residue is computed in Python — the model only writes the note.

Researcher Reads the residue under the proposed frame, or leaves it standing. An analysis with no residue on display should read as suspicious, not finished.

Evolving focus

Trigger The researcher rewrites the question, or the material keeps answering one that wasn't asked.

System Versions it like the story. Machine proposals arrive as a version awaiting adoption, never applied silently. Because focus moves, declined material is parked, not buried — a shift re-offers what was previously out of scope.

Researcher Owns the question at all times; adopting or declining is one action.

Needs judgment

Trigger Derived at read time, never a stored queue.

System Surfaces exceptions only: an unanswered check-back, a finding whose standing went thin, a pending focus proposal, residue with an offer, audio awaiting review.

Researcher Governs by exception. If something can be handled automatically, it never appears here — this is the rule that keeps the tool from becoming a queue of clerical work.

04 · What the researcher touches

Four surfaces, one instrument

TextThe transcript typeset as a document, marginalia in the gutterScrutinize any claim

Decisions happen at the altitude where the researcher actually knows enough. Reactions in a session are fast and cheap. Structural verdicts wait for the sitting, where the pooled evidence is in view — and naming a finding stays unavailable until its evidence has been opened.

05 · What never gets simplified away

The guarantees

These exist because the published critiques of LLM qualitative analysis name specific, reproducible failures. Each guarantee answers one.

GuaranteeHow it holdsAnswers
Verbatim provenanceThe model cites sentence ids; quote text is resolved from character offsets at renderFabricated quotes
Corpus-wide coverageDecile distribution measured per document; front-heavy reads fall back to a finer span“Only the first 2–3 pages”
Persistent statePer-project database; codes, findings and revisions are queryable objectsNo stable codebook
Audit trailAppend-only revision log; nothing destructive; per-project history with restore pointsUnreproducible analysis
Model provenanceThe resolved model recorded per run and in the export manifest“Which model said this?”
Disclosed limitsDeclined material logged with reasons; uncertainty voiced as its own step typeSilent over-claiming
Human gatesNo structural change without a researcher action; naming requires reading the evidenceAutonomous interpretation

What the system claims: reliable application, verbatim provenance, corpus-wide coverage, audited human gates. What it does not claim, because the evidence forbids it: autonomous generation of interpretive themes.

Open questions worth arguing about

Does a check-back run immediately on a steer — one extra call, instant honesty — or ride the next document's synthesis, free but delayed? If a proposed focus revision is ignored rather than declined, should it quietly inform the next read, or stay fully inert? Is “open every passage before naming” still humane at thirty documents, or does the gate need a sampling rule? And the one no architecture answers: for a reviewer who holds that interpretation requires a positioned human subject, no engineering will suffice — only the epistemological reframe, and their acceptance of it.
